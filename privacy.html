Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2, 2026

Welcome to Git Gudder (gitgudder.wiki). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information across our multi-game wiki network.

1. Information We Collect

Git Gudder is a static informational archive. We do not require user accounts, nor do we intentionally collect personal identifying information (PII) from our visitors.

The 100% Item Checklist stores your progress data locally in your own browser via localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us.

2. Hosting & Analytics

This site is hosted via Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may automatically collect aggregated server logs containing basic connection metrics (such as visitor IP addresses and browser types) for operational security and performance purposes. We do not have direct access to individual-level server data. No third-party analytics scripts are loaded on this site.

3. External Links

Our wiki database contains links to external communities, official game pages, and third-party platforms (such as YouTube). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external destinations.

4. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this policy at any time to reflect changes to our hosting or site structure. The effective date above will be updated accordingly.
